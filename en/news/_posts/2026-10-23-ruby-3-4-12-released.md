Ruby 3.4.12 has been released.

This release includes fixes for bugs in Regexp compilation that GitLab's CVE-2026-89078, CVE-2026-93577, CVE-2026-97229 and CVE-2026-110264 rely on.
Please see the topic below for details.

* [Regexp compilation fixes related to GitLab's CVE-2026-89078, CVE-2026-93577, CVE-2026-97229 and CVE-2026-110264]({% link en/news/_posts/2026-10-23-regexp-fixes-for-gitlab-cves.md %})

This release also includes other bugfixes.
Please see the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ruby/ruby/releases/tag/v3_4_12) for further details.
