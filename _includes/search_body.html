{%- comment -%} Marks the element this is placed on as the only region Pagefind indexes, which keeps navigation, the table of contents and the footer out of search results. Pages in a language listed under `search.unsupported` in _config.yml emit nothing here. Once Pagefind has seen a `data-pagefind-body` anywhere on the site it skips every page without one, so leaving the attribute off is what keeps those pages out of the index. lib/search_index.rb then points the language at `search.fallback` so its visitors still get results. A page can opt out on its own with `searchable: false` in its front matter. {%- endcomment -%} {%- unless site.search.unsupported contains page.lang -%} {%- if page.searchable != false -%} data-pagefind-body data-pagefind-meta="title:{{ page.title | strip_html | escape }}" {%- endif -%} {%- endunless -%}
